> Source: `Unique & Check Constraints (2).csv`
> Companion to: `Master_Inspection_Schema.md`
> This is a historical database snapshot and does not include constraints added by Workplan 2 migrations 08 and 09; those migrations have not been applied to a live database by this implementation.
> Conventions:
> - `UNIQUE` constraints are listed by columns (single or composite).
> - `CHECK` constraints include the full `check_clause`.
> - `*_not_null` constraints (auto-generated by PostgreSQL, named `2200_<oid>_<n>_not_null`) are **NOT NULL** enforcement, not value checks. They are summarized per table in the final section rather than repeated individually.

## CHECK Constraints (domain / value rules)

### audit_log
- **audit_log_action_check**: `action IN ('INSERT', 'UPDATE', 'DELETE')`

### backup_log
- **backup_log_backup_type_check**: `backup_type IN ('FULL', 'INCREMENTAL')`
- **backup_log_status_check**: `status IN ('SUCCESS', 'FAILED')`

### base_map_layers
- **base_map_layers_layer_type_check**: `layer_type IN ('SATELLITE', 'STREET', 'LICENSED')`

### critical_machines
- **critical_machines_quantity_check**: `quantity >= 0`

### duplicate_flags
- **duplicate_flags_reason_check**: `reason IN ('NAME_SIMILARITY', 'GPS_PROXIMITY', 'BOTH')`
- **duplicate_flags_resolution_check**: `resolution IN ('OPEN', 'CONFIRMED_DUPLICATE', 'NOT_DUPLICATE', 'MERGED')`

### energy_consumption
- **energy_consumption_share_pct_check**: `share_pct BETWEEN 0 AND 100`

### gis_quality_log
- **gis_quality_log_result_check**: `result IN ('PASS', 'FAIL')`

### industrial_units
- **industrial_units_approval_status_check**: `approval_status IN ('PENDING', 'CONFIRMED', 'RETURNED', 'REJECTED')`
- **industrial_units_establishment_year_check**: `establishment_year BETWEEN 1800 AND 2100`

### machinery_age_profile
- **machinery_age_profile_machine_count_check**: `machine_count >= 0`

### major_inputs
- **major_inputs_cost_share_pct_check**: `cost_share_pct BETWEEN 0 AND 100`

### matrix_items
- **matrix_items_answer_type_check**: `answer_type IN ('CHOICE', 'RATING_1_5')`

### measurement_units
- **measurement_units_unit_kind_check**: `unit_kind IN ('AREA', 'QUANTITY', 'ENERGY', 'POWER', 'OTHER')`

### products
- **products_check**: `product_kind = 'MAIN' OR (revenue_share_pct IS NULL AND hs_code_id IS NULL)`
- **products_installed_capacity_period_check**: `installed_capacity_period IN ('PER_DAY', 'PER_MONTH')`
- **products_product_kind_check**: `product_kind IN ('MAIN', 'SECONDARY')`
- **products_revenue_share_pct_check**: `revenue_share_pct BETWEEN 0 AND 100`

### response_matrix_answers
- **response_matrix_answers_rating_check**: `rating BETWEEN 1 AND 5`
- **response_matrix_answers_check**: `num_nonnulls(choice_value_id, rating) <= 1` (at most one of choice_value_id / rating may be set)

### response_shares
- **response_shares_percent_value_check**: `percent_value BETWEEN 0 AND 100`

### sec_b_operations
- **sec_b_operations_working_days_per_week_check**: `working_days_per_week BETWEEN 1 AND 7`

Migration 09 adds a 1–12 check for `working_months_per_year`. If the historical working-days check is missing in a target database, migration 09 adds it `NOT VALID`; inspect historical rows before validating it.

## Notable Business Rules (agent quick-reference)

- **Percent fields** must be `0–100`: `energy_consumption.share_pct`, `major_inputs.cost_share_pct`, `products.revenue_share_pct`, `response_shares.percent_value`.
- **Non-negative counters**: `critical_machines.quantity`, `machinery_age_profile.machine_count`, `weights_measures_equipment.quantity`, `workforce_*` counts, `sec_i_compliance.safety_trained_workers`.
- **Rating scale** is `1–5` (`response_matrix_answers.rating`) and a row may set *either* `choice_value_id` *or* `rating`, never both.
- **Products**: `SECONDARY` products may not carry `revenue_share_pct` or `hs_code_id`; `MAIN` products may.
- **Photo uploads**: JPEG only, 1 byte–3 MB, valid WGS84 lat/lng, accuracy 0–100 m, only two photo types.
- **Survey response lifecycle**: `DRAFT → PENDING_CONFIRMATION → CONFIRMED | RETURNED | REJECTED`; sync states `PENDING | SYNCED | REJECTED | DUPLICATE`.
- **Unit approval lifecycle**: `PENDING → CONFIRMED | RETURNED | REJECTED` (mirrors `unit_approval_log.action`).
- **Uniqueness of survey answers**: one choice/share/matrix answer per `(response_id, question_id, option_id)` or `(response_id, item_id)`; one row per `(response_id, gender[, skill_level_id])` for workforce tables.
- **Establishment year** bounded `1800–2100`; **working days/week** `1–7`.
